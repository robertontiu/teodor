## Open, honestly
- The far collar still has one heavy line along its outer edge (the shirt's neck opening runs beside it); it reads as one swelling line now, not a slip, but it was not designed. Lesson: proof the key block alone before cutting.
- The hands are still simple mittens with finger lines (ukiyo-e hands are simple, but these could be better drawn).
- The body was not re-posed (it reads seated and asleep; the critic wanted more sag in the shoulders).
