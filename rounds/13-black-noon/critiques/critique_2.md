# Critique 2 (a fresh critic; knew only the theme "Death"; read crit_2.png)

**1) First three seconds:** Dread, then something comic and puzzling. It reads like looking up from a grave or an operating table while strangers peer down at you. The figures look more like masks than mourners.

**2) What causes it:** The worm's-eye view and the ring of faces looking down around a black sun make you the dead person. The phone held over the body adds a cold "filming the death" note, which is the strongest idea in the picture. The screaming figure in red, with an open hand reaching at the viewer, gives the alarm. The faces are tiny dabbed blobs with dot eyes, and that is what pulls it toward cartoon and away from grief.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Composition:** The bright white burst takes up the top half and floats apart from the bottom half. The black disc sits off-center and is too small to carry the meaning.
- **Values:** The top is nearly all one light value. The black coat is one dead flat mass with no form in it. There's almost no middle range linking the two.
- **Edges:** Every edge is the same mushy stamped mark. The faces have odd hard white rims, like cut-out stickers.
- **Drawing:** The heads look like pasted cones with no necks and no bodies leaning in. The hands are good-sized and readable but rubbery. The fingers are stripes.
- **Gesture:** Only the red figure acts. The others hang there, stiff and evenly spaced.
- **Color:** The red and black split is strong. The skin tones are muddy orange, and the yellow ring around the sun looks like a digital artifact.
- **Finish:** The brush texture looks like an automatic filter, the same everywhere, with no clear focal point.

**4) The one change:** Make the circle of watchers believable bodies leaning over the viewer. Give them shoulders and necks that curve inward, put real light on the faces from the bright opening, and let some of them half-dissolve into it. If they become a real group bending over a body, the image reads as death and not as a collage of masks.

(Studio manager's note: dread arrived this time, and the phone filming the death is your strongest idea: give it more weight. The cartoon masks are now what stands between the viewer and the shock: faces need real structure seen from below, necks and shoulders leaning in. Keep the glare and the confrontation.)
