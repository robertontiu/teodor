# Handoff after PHASE 3 (Teodor) - finished

Title: Black Noon. Episodes 1-85 (phase 3: 55-85). Schema versions: 25 (v22-v25 this phase: procedurally shaded heads, then the pulse hand schema).

## Where it ended
- The upper strangers were anatomically impossible (crowns outward, so no neck could attach). Flipped: crowns toward the sun, chins, necks and shoulders out to the frame. All six heads rebuilt from a shaded schema: backlit faces in a lower key, every pair of eyes open with one wet glint, staring straight down; the red woman gasps.
- Black sun enlarged (r 92), acid ring gone, white knife burn round it, broken white rim.
- Phone: bigger, harder glass back with a firing flash, fingers with nails turned toward us.
- Open hand scraped back and re-matched (warmer, painterly); pulse hand rebuilt from a clean schema, the thumb buried so it cannot read as a pistol.

## Lessons for the next painting
- Never draw features with marks (dots, curved lines, parallel strokes): build a shaded schema and match it with small brushes.
- A head against glare is backlit: model it in a low key from the start; glazing down afterwards kills it.
- Glare is not a line: halos and rings appear whenever I draw the glow round a shape.
- Check stroke coordinates on a gridded crop before placing liner accents.
- Two straight fingers plus a raised thumb read as a gun: watch the silhouette of every hand gesture.
