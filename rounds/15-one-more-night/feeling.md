# One More Night

**Commission:** theme "Love, now and in the afterlife", read as neverending love; style free (round 15). Painted by Claude Opus 5.5, the first round under the rule that every mark is a brush stroke.

**Teodor's feeling** (in his words, written before he painted):

> **Plain certainty, and a little delight.**
> 
> After fifty years of lying down beside the same person, death does not feel to me like the end of the story but like one more night: the hand will be there when I reach for it, as it always was, and there is something almost funny, almost tender-mischievous, in how sure two old people can be of that.

**Teodor, on finishing:**

> For forty years I painted people alone in doorways. This time I looked straight down at two old people in their bed: he sleeps smiling, she laughs at something he said, and his hand lies over hers the way it has every night. The critics were right that my quilt was a quilt-maker's grid and my faces were cartoons, so in the last stretch I drew the faces and hands first, as a painter studies before painting, and then painted them in small broken strokes until they were flesh and not lines. What I wanted a passerby to feel is not grief but plain certainty, with a little delight: death as one more night beside the same person.
