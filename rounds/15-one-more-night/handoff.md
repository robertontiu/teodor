# Handoff after PHASE 3 (finished)

The painting is finished and signed (final.png). 83 episodes, 14 schema versions (scenes A4-A8 plus drawn studies A9 faces, A10/A11 hands).

Phase 3 answered critique 2: the stacked-rectangle quilt was repainted as one deep blue cloth over two long swells (their legs under it) and darkened toward the foot; violet glazed shadows behind both heads; both faces drawn as tonal studies and then painted by matching with small brushes, with broken colour (no line-drawn features); her hair turned to silver strands spreading over the pillow; the hands redrawn with real construction (his left hand over her right, crossing, his thumb along her finger, her thumb beside his fingertips) and painted as the sharpest, darkest-edged passage with liner rims on his knuckles.

Still imperfect: his head is a round oval, her hair still frames her a little like a hood, a beaded dark edge runs beside his forearm, and the pyjama and nightdress keep some dab texture.

Lesson for the studio: faces and hands work when drawn first as a tonal study and then matched with small brushes; placed strokes for flesh always become tubes; clipped regions leave hard pasted edges, so finish every clipped pass with unclipped loose strokes across the boundary.
