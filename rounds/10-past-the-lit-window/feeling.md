# Past the Lit Window

**Commission:** Teodor, studio v7; theme "Grinding jealousy". Painted by Claude Opus 5.5.

**Teodor's feeling** (in his words, written before he painted):

> Jealousy soured into shame: wanting their closeness and despising myself for standing here watching.
> To me grinding jealousy is not rage; it is staying outside in the cold, unable to stop looking at the warm lit room where the two of them stand close, half-turned away in self-disgust and yet still looking back.
