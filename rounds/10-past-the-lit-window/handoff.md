## What phase 3 did (answering critique 2)
- The watcher's head redrawn as a clean, hard profile turned back toward the window: forehead, brow, eye notch, nose, a notch of sky, then the fist pressed under the nose (three knuckles), a wrist notch, the shoulder sloping to the house. Warm window rims laid exactly on the contour (they had sat inside it and read as wire). The body still strides left: feet going, head and fist turned back.
- The failed sleeve across the chest was painted out; the arm is imagined.
- Window: corrugated impasto replaced by ~800 broken thick strokes graded cream-to-ochre around the two, kept clear of them; a thin dark frame gives the pane a razor edge while the glow on the wall stays soft; mullion and rail restated; the two refilled as one clean warm dark with a hair of light between her crown and his cheek.
- Sky: top third blended smooth and glazed dark (thin darks, a lid); middle violet streaks calmed; brushwork kept only near the horizon.
- Bottom edge lost in a cold glaze; faint warm glaze on the spill; small finishing lights on the near post and the sill.
