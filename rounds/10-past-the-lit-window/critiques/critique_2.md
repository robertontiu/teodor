# Critique 2 (a fresh blind critic, knew only the theme "Grinding jealousy"; read crit_2.png)

**1) First three seconds:** Lonely exclusion and quiet resignation, with a small sting of envy underneath. My first read is "the outsider walking away" more than "grinding" jealousy. The scene feels melancholy, not corrosive.

**2) What I see:** It's a blue night with a dark figure in a long coat in the left foreground, walking left, away from the house, head turned slightly back. On the right is a low hut with a single bright warm window. Inside it, a couple in silhouette embrace. A fence runs across the middle, and light from the window spills onto the ground.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Drawing/gesture:** The walker's silhouette is stiff and generic. The head reads ambiguously, so you can't tell if they're looking back, and the legs are cut off awkwardly at the base. Nothing in the body *grinds*: no clenched shoulders, no twist toward the window.
- **Value/finish:** The mottled "impasto" filter texture covers everything the same way, sky, wall, window and coat alike. It flattens depth and reads as a digital effect, not paint. It's worst in the window, where it muddies the couple.
- **Edges/color:** All edges are equally soft or mushy. The window should be the one razor-sharp, hot-cool contrast. The large empty sky dilutes the tension.

**Single best fix:** Give the walker a gesture that's torn. The feet should keep walking away while the head and shoulders strain back toward the window, with a sharper jaw and neck line. That inner conflict is what makes it "grinding."
