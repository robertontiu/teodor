# Critique 1 (blind critic, knew only the theme "Exploding rage"; read crit_1.png)

**1) First three seconds:** A heavy, brooding stillness, with a small jolt where the glass cracks. Mostly it feels like sullen, smouldering resentment, a kind of murky dread. It does not read as rage exploding. It reads as rage held in.

**2) What I see:** A dark brown room. A dark figure stands in profile and presses a fist (or its forehead) into a pale grey-blue window with four panes. Thin white crack lines spread out from where it hits. On the floor there is a low clutter of muted violet, blue and grey-green dabs, maybe debris or shattered glass. A few ochre streaks lie on the floor to the right.

**3) Critique:**
- **Drawing:** The figure is shapeless. You can't read the arm, head or fist, so the key act, the strike, is lost. The window's square, dabbed edges read as a digital artifact rather than glass.
- **Value and colour:** Almost everything sits in one narrow band of mid-dark warm brown. The only real contrast is the cool window. Rage gets no hot accent at all: no red, no orange, no saturated colour at the point of impact. The floor debris is pastel and timid.
- **Composition and edges:** Every edge is equally soft and blocky, so nothing is sharp where the violence happens. Everything is static and frontal, and the lower right third is filler.

**Biggest fix:** Make the impact point the hottest, brightest, sharpest thing in the picture. Use jagged, hard-edged shards flying outward with warm light behind them, and give the figure a clear, readable fist and a diagonal thrust. Right now it's a crack in a quiet room. It needs to be an explosion.
