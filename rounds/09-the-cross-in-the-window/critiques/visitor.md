# Visitor critic (final image; knew only the theme "Exploding rage")

1) First three seconds: dread, and a held-in, smothered fury. It reads as anger that has just snapped and is already going cold. The stillness and the brown murk come through before any sense of explosion does.

2) A dark, warm-brown room. A shadowy figure stands at a tall four-pane window with a fist against the glass, right where the crossbars meet. A spiderweb of cracks spreads out from that point, with a small orange-gold glow at the impact. Pale shards float off to the left and are scattered across the floor. The figure is mostly silhouette, with one pale hand hanging at his side.

3) Weaknesses: the scale of the rage (the impact is small, the figure calm; it reads as frustration or despair, the cracks thin and tidy); the figure dissolves into blocky brown sludge, and the boxy stroke artifacts look like a digital filter rather than gesture; the muddy umber damps the heat, with no red or hot orange beyond a tiny spark.
Strongest: the cold blue-white window against the warm dark interior, and the radial crack pattern centered on the fist, together a clear, readable focal point and a strong idea.
