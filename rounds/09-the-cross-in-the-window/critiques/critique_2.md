# Critique 2 (a fresh blind critic, knew only the theme "Exploding rage"; read crit_2.png)

**1) First three seconds:** It feels like a heavy, brooding moment just after the act, more aftermath than explosion. Under that there's a dull, smouldering anger: a mood that has already gone cold instead of rage going off.

**2) What I see:** A dark silhouetted figure, seen from behind, stands in a brown-orange room. One fist is pressed against the crossing bars of a tall, cold blue-grey window. White cracks spread out from the point of impact. The other hand hangs down, maybe holding something. Pale debris or shards lie on the floor, and the right half of the canvas is an almost empty ochre wall.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Drawing:** The figure is a soft blob. The punching arm has no shoulder, elbow or tension, and the body is static. That is the posture of someone leaning, not striking.
- **Value and composition:** The only strong contrast is the window, and the figure sits passively against it. The right 40% is muddy mid-value brown with nothing happening there. The cracks are thin and polite.
- **Edges and finish:** The blocky square dabs are the same everywhere. The window edges are smeared, the mullion is blurred, and the floor reads as noise. Nothing is crisp, so nothing hits.

**The one fix:** Make the moment of impact violent. Draw a sharp, angular fist with the arm and body coiled behind it. Make the glass burst outward with hard-edged shards in the air and bright light spilling in. Then crop or darken the empty right side so the whole painting pulls toward that point.
