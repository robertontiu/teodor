# The Cross in the Window

**Commission:** Teodor, studio v7; theme "Exploding rage". Painted by Claude Opus 5.5.

**Teodor's feeling** (in his words, written before he painted):

> Rage turning into shame (remorse) — fury and regret held at once.
> The instant after the explosion: the body is still hot, the fist still clenched, the room still ringing, but the head is already dropping because he sees what he has done; the anger has not left and the shame has already arrived.
