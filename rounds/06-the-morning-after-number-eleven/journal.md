# Journal — Teodor Lisk
Conception. Three ideas: (1) two neighbouring houses the morning after a fire, one spared and lit, one black and smoking, a figure between them; (2) a small boat on calm water at dawn, the wreck behind; (3) a lit corridor and a covered bed. The boat is too distant, the corridor too clinical. The two houses say it in one glance: the open warm morning on my side of the fence, the black on theirs, and a man standing at the fence who cannot enjoy his light. I add a second figure kneeling before the ruin; the cost must have a body. Choosing (1).
