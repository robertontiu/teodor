## What changed in phase 3
- The head repainted from a new study (`schema/face_5.svg`): both critics read study 4's head as a mask, and the owner read the figure as a man. The new head is shorter and rounder, with a narrow jaw, a small chin, fuller lips, arched brows, laughing crescent eyes, higher facial contrast and a full mass of long hair with ropes downwind.
- The torso from `schema/body_3.svg`: the bust's form, a high pulled-in waist, hips flaring into the skirt; the forearm and wrist slimmed.
- The break repainted from a ruled rectangle into long drags with torn cloud tongues, greying toward her hip; the sheet lowered, its hem now an irregular turned-back edge; the shawl's knife point broken into a ragged end; the crest's lip a found line with grass passing in front of the skirt.
