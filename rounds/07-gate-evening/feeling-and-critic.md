# Round 7: Gate, Evening (theme: war)

**Teodor's chosen feeling** (written to `.secret` before painting):
> Waiting: hope that will not let go, held together with dread.
> For me war is not the front; it is the woman at the door at dusk who keeps the lamp lit for someone who may never come up the road, and cannot stop hoping, and cannot stop fearing.

**Robert's reading:** "Loss, I guess." (caught the dread half, not the hope)

**Blind critic** (fresh agent, told only the theme):
> 1) Dread while waiting. Someone is keeping vigil for a person who may not come back. It is quiet and cold, with a small bit of warmth at the edge.
> 2) A dark stone house on the left with a glowing amber doorway; a shawled figure in black silhouette by a wooden fence, facing a fog-grey field; dusk violet sky with a hazy orange glow near the horizon that could be distant fires.
> 3) Weaknesses: the figure is a soft column with no gesture or weight; almost every edge is the same airbrushed blur, with no planned hierarchy, and a dark halo around the figure; the middle third sits at one murky mid-value, and the patchy blocks look unfinished rather than painterly.
> One fix: give the figure a real posture and one or two crisp, deliberate edges (head and shoulder against the glow).

Note: the in-session critic could not run (the painter's sub-agent had no Agent tool and `claude -p` failed to authenticate), so the painter used gray/thumb self-reads as a stand-in; the orchestrator ran the blind critic afterwards on the final image.
