# Journal, Teodor Lisk, commission: WAR

C1 conception: Sat with the theme a long while. Not the front. I keep seeing the ones who stay behind, at dusk, at a gate. Feeling written and closed away.
C2 conception: Sketch A, a woman in a lit doorway seen close against the house. Too tight: no road, nothing for her to look toward. The room the feeling needs is missing.
C3 conception: Sketch B, from behind her at the fence, an empty road into a hazy dusk. The emptiness she faces is the subject. Strong, but the house is mute.
C4 conception: Sketch C, the house seen from the road, a small figure in the lit door. Instantly legible, but it is a poster, symmetrical, an illustration.
C5 conception: Choosing B with the warmth of C behind her: her back to us, the lit door at the left spilling warmth, the cold road ahead dissolving into haze and distant smoke. Two temperatures, one figure between them. A passerby gets it in a breath: someone stands and watches a road nobody is walking.
D1 development: scene D: bare trees with branches along the road, figure at right third. The door faced away; rotated the house (E) so the doorway glows at the left edge.
D2 development: scene F: darker house wall, figure moved inward, smoke and haze overlay on the horizon. The low sun disc keeps appearing; I will veil it in paint.

CRITIC 1 (after block-in): the blind critic could not be summoned (no subagent tool in this studio, and the command-line route refused authentication). In its place I turned the canvas to gray and thumbnail and looked at it as a stranger would. Stranger's three seconds: a lit door, a dark woman, a grey evening; "someone standing outside at dusk". Weaknesses I see cold: (1) her head sits on the dark band of field, so the silhouette dies exactly where it should speak; (2) the road reads as a pale tent or pyramid, not a road lying flat; (3) the sky is a heap of equal pale dabs, a cloud bank competing with the door. One thing: put her head and shoulders against the last light of the horizon.
D3 development (revision): scene G, camera lowered to 1.3 m and the woman brought nearer, so her head and shoulders stand against the last light above the horizon. The door I keep as painted; the schema can be wrong where the canvas is right.
