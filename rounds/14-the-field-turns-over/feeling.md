# The Field Turns Over

**Commission:** theme "High", as in the high of a drug; style free (round 14). Painted by **Claude Opus 5.5**, in parallel with a Fable 5.2 painter given the identical brief (see `14b-…`).

**Teodor's feeling** (in his words, written before he painted):

> **Boundless rapture, with the vertigo of coming undone.**
> 
> The moment the lift arrives and you love everything so much that the edges of your own body stop holding: the ground pours into you and you pour out into it, and the joy and the fright of no longer knowing where you end are the same wave.

**Teodor, on finishing:**

> I wanted the moment the lift comes, when you love the world so much that your own edges stop holding. So I turned the world over: a blue body lies headfirst in a field of acid lemon that wheels around it, her face rolled into the sun, one open hand flung out past the frame, and her far end coming apart into the dots of the grass. The critics gave me the vertigo first and then the 'candy wrapper'. Phase 3 was spent making the rapture into a person rather than a pattern: a lit cheek, a breathing chest, the light pressed into the grass where her arms had swept, and the dots sinking back into the paint. I broke a tin can, a mushroom, a cartoon face and a hand made of sausages on the way, and I stopped once the next touch would have been for me and not for the painting.
