# Critique 1 (a fresh critic; knew only the theme "High", as in the high of a drug; read crit_1.png)

**1) First three seconds:** Euphoric, but it reads as a party. Confetti and a festival, with arms thrown up. It's more celebration than chemical high.

**2) Why:** The large blue figure with arms flung wide, the head tipped back and the body pushed off-centre to the right reads as surrender and release. The acid chartreuse ground with hot pink and blue dots gives a buzzing, overstimulated hum. The dots drifting over the body help a little: the figure seems to be dissolving into the air.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Composition:** The figure is crammed against the right edge and cropped at the top, so the energy runs out of the frame. The left half is a large empty field of evenly spread dots with no destination or rhythm.
- **Values:** Almost everything sits in the same mid-light band. Only the dark blue legs and the shadow behind the shoulder give real depth. Nothing glows, so there's no "rush."
- **Edges:** The figure's outline is uniformly mushy, and the dots are uniformly hard. Nothing is chosen.
- **Drawing/gesture:** There are three or four arms (two raised, two outstretched) that read as a mistake rather than as motion blur. The torso is a stiff tube, the head is barely there, and the legs merge into the dark.
- **Color:** Lime against pink is loud but flat and decorative, with no temperature shift around the figure.
- **Finish:** The dots look stamped on like a filter, all the same size, laid over the paint instead of coming out of it.

**4) The one change:** Build a real light source. Put a hot, near-white bloom behind or above the head and let it bleach the surrounding colors and blow out the edges of the figure, while the lower body and the left field darken. Light coming from the figure is what turns "party" into "high."

(Studio manager's note: the critic read a standing figure, not one lying in the grass seen from above, and it does read as a figure on a cross at a glance: the lying-down view and the ghost arms need to read as what you mean. Their point about values stands: nothing glows. But a radiant bloom behind the head is one of your listed habits (the central burst): if you want glow, find it another way. "Party, not high" is the reading to beat: the vertigo of coming undone has no form yet.
