# Visitor (a fresh critic; knew only the theme "High"; read final.png, then the contact sheet of past work)

**1) What I feel in the first three seconds:** Weightless, a sunny kind of tipsy, a body falling or floating. It reads as cheerful vertigo more than a drug high.

**2) What causes it:** The figure is upside down and on a diagonal, with one arm flung out, so it seems to drop into a vortex. The acid yellow-green background has concentric dotted rings (pink and white) that pull the eye into a spiral. Cool cobalt against hot chartreuse makes the colors buzz. The upper body breaks up into confetti dots, which hints at a self coming apart.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Composition:** The figure is cut off on three edges: the head at the top-left, the arm at the right and the leg at the bottom. The vortex centre (lower right) and the figure fight for attention. The cream patches at the right and lower left are dead holes.
- **Values:** The figure is almost all mid-blue and the ground almost all mid-yellow. There is no real dark or real light, so the form flattens.
- **Edges:** Uniformly soft and mushy. The body reads like a smeared 3D render.
- **Drawing:** The anatomy is unclear. The torso, hip and pelvis area is ambiguous and the hand is a blob. You can't tell at once which way the body faces.
- **Gesture:** Stiff, more mannequin than ecstatic surrender.
- **Color:** Loud but one-note. No temperature shifts inside the blue body.
- **Finish:** The digital look takes over. The stamp-like rectangular strokes, filter-made dot rings and plastic shading look like separate effects, not one surface.

**4) The one change:** Redraw the figure with a clear, legible gesture (head back, spine arched), set inside the frame, with a true dark and a true highlight. The vortex then has something solid to act on.

**5) Against past work:** A real departure in palette and mood. The contact sheet is mostly dark, amber-lit and solemn: lone silhouettes in doorways, at windows or at dusk. This one is high-key, acid and playful. It still repeats things:
- a lone central figure
- a radial burst or tunnel around the subject (like 03-after-the-supper-plates, 11-the-long-gold-hour and 13-black-noon)
- a vertigo or overhead viewpoint (like 13-black-noon)
- the same soft, smeared digital handling and signature placement
