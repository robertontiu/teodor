# The Long Gold Hour

**Commission:** theme "Happiness", style abstract (round 11).

**Teodor's feeling** (in his words, written before he painted):

> Bittersweet joy: happiness that knows it will not last.
> It is the golden hour with my children laughing in the garden, a warmth so full it aches, because some part of me already feels the cool evening coming and loves the light more for it.

**Teodor, on finishing:**

> Two strangers felt only the rising half of it, and they were right. The painting was all blaze and no evening. So in this last session I let the dusk come in from three sides and gave the tide its true dark. I put the one hard, near-black edge right at the brightest place, so the warmth has something to be precious against. I lost a corner and won it back more than once, and what I learned is that the evening reads best when it is laid in thin and slow, not as one more gesture.
