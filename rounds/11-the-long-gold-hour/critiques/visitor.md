# Visitor (a fresh critic; knew only the commission "Happiness", abstract; read final.png)

**1) First three seconds:** Warm, lifted, and moving fast. It reads more as exhilaration and release, a burst of light breaking through, than as quiet contentment. Underneath that there is a little vertigo.

**2) What causes it:** The glowing cream-white core, with orange and gold radiating from it. The strong diagonal sweep from upper left to lower right. The streaky, wind-blown strokes all running the same way. The deep blue-violet band at the bottom and the plum at the upper right frame the light, so it seems to burst out of darkness.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Composition:** It is one big diagonal band with no counter-movement, so it looks like a tilted sunset photo or a motion-blurred screenshot. The upper-right plum block is filled with repetitive stamped dashes and feels like a separate, unresolved zone.
- **Values:** The jump from light to dark is mostly a soft gradient. Apart from the stray black dash near the centre, there are no crisp darks inside the light, so the core lacks punch.
- **Edges:** Almost everything is either airbrush-soft or uniformly streaky. There are no deliberate hard edges, so nothing anchors the eye.
- **Color:** Pleasant but expected, a sunset palette. The orange and violet turn muddy and mauve where they meet, especially to the right of the core.
- **Mark-making:** The strokes are uniform in size, direction and pressure, so they read as a digital brush preset. The thin "scribble" lines look mechanical.
- **Finish:** The black dash near the centre and the pale streak low in the image look like accidents. The signature is fine.

**4) The one change:** Break the single diagonal. Add one bold, decisive counter-gesture through or out of the light core, such as a thick upward arc in saturated yellow or white with a hard edge. That gives the eye a focal point and makes the energy feel joyful instead of windswept.
