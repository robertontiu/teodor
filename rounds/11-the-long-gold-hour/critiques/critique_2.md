# Critique 2 (a fresh critic; knew only the commission "Happiness", abstract; read crit_2.png)

**1) First three seconds:** warmth, a rush, a sunrise bursting out. It reads as elation, and also a bit like a weather event.

**2) What causes it:** The glowing cream-yellow core in the upper middle radiates into orange and magenta, so it feels like a sun or something opening up. The strokes sweep diagonally from upper-left to lower-right, which gives speed and lift. The cool violet-blue mass in the bottom-left adds contrast that makes the warmth glow.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Composition:** It reads as a sunset landscape (sky, sun, dark hill) more than an abstraction, which is a cliché. Every stroke runs the same diagonal, so the energy is uniform and nothing pushes against it. The corners, especially the top right and bottom right, are leftover filler.
- **Values:** The range sits in the middle. There's no true dark accent anywhere, so the light core lacks punch. The blue mass is mid-value too.
- **Edges:** Nearly all edges are soft or hatched the same way. There's no crisp, deliberate edge to anchor the eye. The thin violet streak near the center is the only sharp moment, and it feels accidental.
- **Color:** It's pretty, but it's the stock sunset palette. The orange-to-blue transition along the bottom turns muddy and magenta.
- **Mark-making:** Repetitive digital hatching with the same width, pressure and direction everywhere. It reads as a filter, not a hand. The bumpy "impasto" texture in the core looks like a plastic artifact.
- **Finish:** Busy all over without any area of rest. It feels unresolved rather than intentionally loose.

**4) The one change:** Break the uniformity at the focal point. Add a few bold, decisive strokes against the main diagonal (or upward-rising ones), with hard edges and one near-black or saturated accent right beside the light core. That gives the joy a gesture and a peak, instead of a smooth gradient of pleasantness.
