# Critique 1 (blind critic, knew only the theme "Happiness" and that it is abstract; read crit_1.png)

**1) First three seconds:** Radiant warmth and uplift, like sun on my face. Right behind it comes a little vertigo: the whole scene tilts down to the right, so the joy feels as if it's sliding off the canvas. It reads as elation, not calm contentment.

**2) What I see:** A blazing white-yellow sun-burst, a little above and right of center. It is wrapped in thick orange and coral dabs that radiate outward. Short dark-orange strokes on the left read as sun rays or flames. A diagonal band of magenta-pink separates the sky from a deep blue-violet "sea" in the lower third, which is laid in with horizontal dashes and has a few small orange glints. It reads as a sunset over water, seen at a tilt.

**3) Weaknesses:**
- **Color and value:** The upper two-thirds sits almost entirely in mid-high orange. The sun barely separates from its surroundings, and the dark accents sit only in the blue band, so the top looks flat and saturated with no resting place.
- **Edges and mark-making:** The marks are mostly uniform rounded "sausage" dabs, all with the same soft edge. The left mass looks smeared and blurry rather than intentional, and there are no crisp edges near the sun to give it a focal point.
- **Composition and rhythm:** The sea is a heavy dark wedge that pulls the eye down and out at the bottom-left. Upward strokes and diagonal strokes fight each other.

**Most important fix:** Give the sun's core real light against the dark: add a few cooler or deeper notes (violet or crimson) close to it, and sharpen 2–3 edges right at the core. Joy needs contrast to glow.
