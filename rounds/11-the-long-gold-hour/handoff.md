## What each element does for the feeling (see .secret)
- The light: the fullness. Generous, still at its heart; only its edges stream away.
- The violet reaching in: the coming evening already inside the warmth, gently, not striking. The ache lives at that tip.
- The lilac tail of the light: the warmth cooling at its own end as it leans away.
- Dusk at the edges: the embrace that is also a closing.
- The seam: the last light holding on at the edge where the cool arrives.
- The tide: what is coming, slow, heavy, inevitable, not threatening.
- The single diagonal: one breath, everything leaning the same way, passing.

## Technical lessons this phase
- blend makes pale sludge-blobs in this painting; avoid it. Bristle (fan free strokes along the lean, spread <= 5, curve <= 0.04) heals blobs.
- fan strokes are translucent and do not cover: to remove a mark, a loaded round first, then a fan pull through it.
- a fan match over a region from the schema gives good bristle but follows the schema's gradient (concentric swirl around the core); follow it with free fan strokes on the lean.
- glazes over dry paint (dry 0.7 first) are the best way to unify busy passages and to deepen the edges; a feathered warm-white glaze makes light glow from within.
- separate saturated stripes read as clouds/things; keep color shifts within one stroke family.
