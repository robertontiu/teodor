# Work in progress: Teodor v8 (styles, media, finishing, research)

Started 2026-11-25 from Robert's notes after Spectre (github.com/robertontiu/spectre) outgrew Teodor in output quality. This file is the plan and its state, so a fresh session can pick up if this one stops. Tick items as they land.

## Robert's five points
1. **Research:** ground the painting in research on how emotion is carried by and perceived in images, as Spectre is grounded in music-emotion research.
2. **Human-analogous process:** compare with Spectre and fill the gaps with more research.
3. **Style as configuration:** a style is layered (current/movement × medium × support × technique × finishing), configured as sourced data, with research for unknown styles part of the process; the engine gains media and tools like Spectre gains instruments.
4. **Finishing as mastering:** research what the last stage is per medium and per current; build it.
5. **Styles, never authors:** only styles and movements are versioned. A request for a specific artist may get temporary research and a local configuration in `studio/styles.local/` (gitignored), never committed.

Plus anything else from Spectre that applies.

## After round 16 (the first v8 painting)
- [x] **A pre-checkpoint self-check in the brief:** (in `docs/prompts/painter-brief.md` since round 17) before `crit_N.png`, the painter checks the canvas against the repeat offenders: features painted rather than drawn, more than one stroke shape and size, the cause of an action visible, no bare-ground holes, no empty block. That way the critic's reading goes to the feeling.
- [x] **A stroke vocabulary that doesn't read as a filter:** every critic in every round names the uniform dab. That calls for strokes that change width, pressure and loading along their length, broken-colour and scumble passes, and size tied to distance and role. (2026-11-26) Built in the core stroke path (`play_v8`, `Drift`, `FormField`, `match`/`free`/`strokes`/`model`; oil-v7 untouched and still bit-identical): articulations with pressure, width, load and dry-brush profiles along the stroke and a slow pressure wave; size, pressure, load, angle, colour and edge drifting smoothly across a passage (Hennig et al. 2011, correlated rather than independent variation); size by distance from the declared focus and by role; strokes following a form field (structure tensor of the schema, nearest form, then a slow flow), deviating from it by a drifting angle; stroke edges by role. Measured (same blockout, same four `match` episodes, oil on canvas; `eyes.mark_stats`; 14 public-domain Met oils from `studio/calibration_refs.json`):

  Round 16 (v8 before this work) had a whole-picture direction spread of 0.14: strokes at one angle everywhere, which `report` now flags. The direction measures moved into the Met range; local coherence moved a little toward it. Still far off: stroke-scale coherence (bristle furrows make every stroke internally parallel) and stroke-scale edge variety (the rendered oil surface has little micro-contrast compared with photographs of real impasto); both live in the medium's render, not the core hand. Sheets: `docs/gallery/core_strokes_before.jpg`, `core_strokes_after.jpg`. All profile shapes, mixes and multipliers are eye.
