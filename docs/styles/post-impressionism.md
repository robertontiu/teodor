# Post-Impressionism

Researched 2026-11. Every value names its source; values marked **judgement** have no published source and are editorial choices for Teodor.

## 1. In one paragraph
Post-Impressionism is a retrospective umbrella (coined in 1910) for French painting of roughly 1885-1905 that grew out of Impressionism's light palette and broken touch but wanted more: structure, method, and expression. It is not one style, so this configuration names three **modes**, and a commission picks one (or Teodor does):
- **constructive**: form built from patches and parallel hatched strokes of modulated colour, "one touch laid across another, even as a builder works" ([Phillips Collection, *Up Close with Cézanne*](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf));
- **divisionist** (pointillism): prismatic colours "minimally mixed", applied as "small dots, dabs and dashes ... next to one another without blending" ([ColourLex, quoting the AIC / Munsell study of *La Grande Jatte*](https://colourlex.com/project/georges-seurat-a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte/));
- **expressive** (contour and colour): complementaries "boldly exaggerated" to convey a feeling ([Van Gogh Museum, Vincent's Colours](https://vangoghmuseum.nl/en/stories/looking-for-contrast)), impasto, and the flat colour planes inside dark outlines of Cloisonnism and Synthetism ([The Art Story, Cloisonnism and Synthetism](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/cloisonnism-and-synthetism/); [Musée de Pont-Aven on Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/3AXB4iaqB_MqNg)).
Paul Cézanne, Georges Seurat, Vincent van Gogh, Paul Gauguin and Émile Bernard are named only as historical context.

## 2. How it carries feeling
- **Colour as feeling, not record.** A painter "should not attempt to reproduce the exact colour he saw"; "the effects colours produce through their harmonies or discords should be boldly exaggerated" to convey a feeling. Clashing reds, greens and yellows made a café "a harsh place"; red ochre, "green saddened with grey" and black outlines gave "the feeling of anxiety" ([Van Gogh Museum](https://vangoghmuseum.nl/en/stories/looking-for-contrast)). Complementaries placed side by side "appear more visually powerful than when isolated" (same).
- **Surface as temperament.** Impasto concentrated colour and added "emotion and movement"; "violent" moods required more impasto, calmer ones thinner paint ([Getty, The Emotion behind Van Gogh's Brushstrokes](https://www.getty.edu/news/impasto-paint-how-to-van-gogh-art-style-technique)). This maps directly onto Teodor's felt state.
- **Synthesis and deformation.** Pont-Aven painters "deformed these images in order to more clearly express certain moods" ([Musée de Pont-Aven](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/3AXB4iaqB_MqNg)); Synthetism is "an inward emotional and spiritual turn ... in favor of feeling and symbolism" ([The Art Story](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/cloisonnism-and-synthetism/)).
- **Order and permanence** (constructive and divisionist modes): stillness, measured calm, a luminosity "that has the property of light sources" ([ColourLex / AIC](https://colourlex.com/project/georges-seurat-a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte/)). **Judgement:** these modes carry contemplation and gravity, not agitation.
- Avoided: Impressionism's fleeting snapshot; academic modelling; neutral mixed greys.

## 3. Media, supports and grounds
- **Oil on canvas** (default). Watercolour over graphite also belongs to the constructive mode (Cézanne's related watercolours: [Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)); conté crayon tonal drawing belongs to the divisionist mode (**judgement**: well known, not verified in a fetched source here).
- **Ground:** white, left visible "throughout the canvas" in the constructive mode ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)); the Seurat border was laid over a lead-white underpaint ([AIC, La Grande Jatte frame by frame](https://www.artic.edu/articles/969/la-grande-jatte-frame-by-frame)). Teodor default `#f0ebdf` (**judgement**, a warm white).

## 4. Palette
- **Constructive:** chrome and cadmium yellows, Naples yellow, yellow ochre, red ochre, vermilion, madder lake, alizarin, cobalt, ultramarine, Prussian blue, emerald and viridian, zinc white, peach black ([eclecticlight, pigments after 1850](https://eclecticlight.co/2015/10/06/pigments-technique-%e2%86%92-style-4-after-1850/), secondary). Hues of blue, green, yellow and orange hatched from thin washes to richer layers ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)). Hex (judgement): `#f0ebdf` white, `#e6b23a` chrome yellow, `#c99a4b` ochre, `#b5553a` red ochre, `#3d5fa8` cobalt, `#2c3f7a` ultramarine, `#3f7d5e` viridian, `#7a8a4a` olive mixture, `#8c6aa0` violet.
- **Divisionist:** "solely of prismatic colors that were minimally mixed"; iron oxide yellow, burnt sienna and black abandoned; zinc yellow, chrome yellow, vermilion and red lake added; border palette vermilion, red lake, cadmium yellow, chrome yellow, cobalt blue, lead white ([ColourLex / AIC](https://colourlex.com/project/georges-seurat-a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte/)). Darks from ultramarine + red lake, very little black (same). Hex (judgement): `#f3eee2` white, `#f4dc3c` zinc yellow (as applied, before it browned), `#eab21e` chrome yellow, `#df4a2e` vermilion, `#b8345a` red lake, `#45b07a` emerald, `#2f58b0` cobalt, `#2a3488` ultramarine, `#e9965a` orange mixture.
- **Expressive:** chrome yellow, geranium lake, carmine, red lead, cobalt and ultramarine, Prussian blue, emerald and viridian, bone black ([eclecticlight](https://eclecticlight.co/2015/10/06/pigments-technique-%e2%86%92-style-4-after-1850/)); complementary pairs purple/yellow, orange/green, blue/yellow, red/green ([Van Gogh Museum](https://vangoghmuseum.nl/en/stories/looking-for-contrast)). Hex (judgement): `#efb81c` chrome yellow, `#e8722a` orange, `#d6343a` geranium, `#2d4fa8` cobalt, `#1f3d6e` Prussian, `#3a9a5a` emerald, `#6e4a9a` violet, `#1a1a22` outline black.
- **Avoid:** mud from mixing complementaries on the palette (the trap Van Gogh's early work fell into: [Van Gogh Museum](https://vangoghmuseum.nl/en/stories/looking-for-contrast)); brown tonal unity; varnish-gold.

## 5. Light and value
- Middle-to-high key, light from colour rather than chiaroscuro (**judgement**, following the white grounds and prismatic palettes).
- Divisionist: luminosity from high-chroma light pigments; haloing at form edges, alternating stroke size, type and direction ([AIC frame by frame](https://www.artic.edu/articles/969/la-grande-jatte-frame-by-frame); [ColourLex](https://colourlex.com/project/georges-seurat-a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte/)).
- Constructive: late-afternoon shadows rendered by palette, planes "flat translucent geometric" ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)).
- Expressive: light symbolic (lamp light, gas light: [Van Gogh Museum](https://vangoghmuseum.nl/en/stories/looking-for-contrast)); flat-toned colour with little shading in Synthetism ([Pont-Aven](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/3AXB4iaqB_MqNg)).

## 6. Drawing and form
- **Constructive:** pencil sketch, then outlines with "a fine pointed brush and blue paint", then form built of hatched strokes and colour patches ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)); forms reduced to planes.
- **Divisionist:** contours enlarged or added to figures in the dot campaign ([ColourLex / AIC](https://colourlex.com/project/georges-seurat-a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte/)); stiff, geometric, frieze-like figures (**judgement**).
- **Expressive:** "black lines that define the outlines" ([Van Gogh Museum](https://vangoghmuseum.nl/en/stories/looking-for-contrast)); Cloisonnism combined "large, flat color planes with heavy black outlines"; simplified shapes and "lofty perspective" ([The Art Story](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/cloisonnism-and-synthetism/); [Pont-Aven](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/3AXB4iaqB_MqNg)). Deformation for mood is allowed.

## 7. Composition
- Constructive: stable, architectural; edges may be left less complete than the centre ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)).
- Divisionist: planned, frieze-like, with a painted inner border that transitions to the frame ([AIC](https://www.artic.edu/articles/969/la-grande-jatte-frame-by-frame)).
- Expressive: high viewpoint, flattened depth, Japanese-print influence: "strongly defined outlines and areas of even colour" and "lack of perspectival depth" ([The Art Story](https://www.theartstory.org/movement/cloisonnism-and-synthetism/)).

## 8. Marks and edges
- **Constructive:** thin parallel hatched strokes and thick colour patches, from thin washes to rich layers; edges built by adjacent planes, contour restated in blue ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf); "constructive stroke": [eclecticlight](https://eclecticlight.co/2015/10/06/pigments-technique-%e2%86%92-style-4-after-1850/)). Strokes all at one diagonal in a passage (**judgement**).
- **Divisionist:** dots, dabs and dashes, unblended ([ColourLex](https://colourlex.com/project/georges-seurat-a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte/)); size and direction alternate at edges (haloing: [AIC](https://www.artic.edu/articles/969/la-grande-jatte-frame-by-frame)).
- **Expressive:** directional impasto strokes, wet into wet ("quickly and without letting one layer of paint dry": [AIC microscopy, *The Poet's Garden*](https://archive.artic.edu/sciarttech/2e3.html)); long lines for water, dots for foliage, broad strokes for sky ([Van Gogh Museum](https://vangoghmuseum.nl/en/stories/looking-for-contrast)); hard dark contour lines around flat fields (Cloisonnism).

## 9. Process (the order of work)
**Constructive** ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)): 1. pencil sketch [schema + `transfer`]; 2. outline in thin blue with a fine brush [`liner`]; or start from daubs of a thin brown wash (later work); 3. build with hatched strokes from thin washes to richer layers across the whole [phase 2]; 4. rework the focal area with impasto (the eyes of a self-portrait were reworked several times) [phase 3]; 5. stop with white ground visible and edges less resolved.
**Divisionist** ([ColourLex / AIC](https://colourlex.com/project/georges-seurat-a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte/); [AIC](https://www.artic.edu/articles/969/la-grande-jatte-frame-by-frame)): 1. first campaign in an Impressionist-like technique with earths and a little black [phase 1-2]; 2. second campaign: dots of minimally mixed prismatic colour over the whole, contours enlarged or added [phase 2-3]; 3. third campaign: a painted border in a limited palette, on lead-white underpaint [finishing].
**Expressive:** 1. drawing of the main contours [`transfer` / `liner`]; 2. lay in flat or directional colour fields, wet into wet ([AIC microscopy](https://archive.artic.edu/sciarttech/2e3.html)); 3. impasto in proportion to the intensity of feeling ([Getty](https://www.getty.edu/news/impasto-paint-how-to-van-gogh-art-style-technique)); 4. restate the dark outlines last (**judgement**).

## 10. Finishing
- Constructive: "finished" could mean unfinished: white ground visible, edges incomplete ([Phillips](https://phillipscollection.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/up-close-with-cezanne-text-panels.pdf)). No unifying glaze; varnishes found on these paintings yellowed and have been removed (same).
- Divisionist: the painted border is the finishing stage; a white frame instead of gold ([AIC](https://www.artic.edu/articles/969/la-grande-jatte-frame-by-frame)).
- Expressive: last outlines and accents; no varnish tone (**judgement**).

## 11. Pitfalls for Teodor
- **Swirl pastiche:** starry skies, sunflowers, uniform swirls everywhere. Stroke direction must follow this subject's forms, and swirls are a mode, not a signature (**judgement**).
- **Dot-filter:** pointillism as an even dot texture over a finished image. The real process puts dots over a painted first campaign, varies dot size at edges and chooses complementaries.
- **Cézanne-as-cubes:** geometric faceting without colour modulation.
- **Mud:** mixing complementaries instead of placing them side by side.
- **Nearest wrong feelings:** decorative cheerfulness (expressive), sterile system (divisionist), Teodor's hushed bittersweet habit.
- Engine: a dot or hatch with a fixed angle is not a native intention; `free` with short `length` and narrow `spread` approximates both.

## 12. Engine mapping
- **Constructive (oil, exists):** `new "#f0ebdf"`; `transfer` faint; `liner` in blue for outlines; `free` with `flat`, size 12-20, `length` [20, 45], `direction {"angle": 60, "spread": 6}`, low `wetmix` (0.2), `cover` 0.85 so ground shows; `knife`/high `impasto` only in the focal area. Felt bias: arousal -0.1, tension 0.1, care 0.4.
- **Divisionist (oil, exists):** first campaign `do_match` with `flat` size 25-40; `dry`; then `free` with `round` size 5-9, `length` [4, 8], `count` high, `wetmix` 0.05, `pickup` 0.05, `color_jitter` 0.0, colours given as complementary pairs per region; border as a `rect` region. Felt bias: arousal -0.3, tension 0.0, care 0.6.
- **Expressive (oil, exists):** `free` with `round`/`flat`, `direction {"field": true}` or `around`, `impasto` 0.6-0.9 scaled by arousal, `wetmix` 0.7; `liner` in dark blue-black for outlines; flat fields with `flat` size 40+ and low jitter. Felt bias: arousal 0.4, tension 0.3, care 0.0.
- **Watercolour (being added):** needed for the constructive mode's watercolour practice.
- **Gaps:** no native dot or hatch primitive; no colour rule that places complementaries; `do_match` would blend away divisionist dots, so dots must be `free`; zinc yellow's browning is not modelled (and should not be).
